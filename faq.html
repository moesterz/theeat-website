Getting started
When you sign up, Eat asks a few quick questions about your goals, the foods you love and avoid, your stores and your weekly budget. It then builds a full week of meals around those answers — and reshapes future weeks as your tastes and goals change.

Plans & pricing
Solo starts with a 7-day free trial, so you won't be charged until it ends, and you can cancel anytime from the App Store. Power begins right away. See full details on the pricing page.
Solo gives you a personalised plan, weekly shopping list and smart swaps. Power adds macro & calorie coaching and the restaurant menu helper for eating out — and switches the app to its blue Power identity.
Anytime. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your App Store subscription settings. Your meal history and saved recipes stay with your account.
Shopping & everyday use
Eat turns your week into one list, grouped by aisle and matched to your stores and budget. Swap or skip a meal and the list updates instantly. In regions where we don't have local prices yet, you'll get the full list without prices — and we'll add them as they roll out.
