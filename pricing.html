Questions about billing

How do I use a web subscription in the app?
Sign in on your iPhone with the same account you used here. Your plan is already there.
Is the price the same as in the app?
Monthly is the same everywhere. Yearly prices are set separately on the web and in the App Store, and you always see the exact amount before you pay.
Can I cancel?
Any time. Web subscriptions are managed from your account; App Store subscriptions from your iPhone's Settings. You keep access until the end of the period.
