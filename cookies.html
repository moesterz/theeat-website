01 What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help a site or app remember you. On theeat.app we use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and understand how the site is used.

The Eat mobile app itself does not use advertising cookies. This policy mainly covers our website.

02 Types we use

Essential

  • Keep you signed in and secure during a session.
  • Remember choices like your billing toggle and plan selection.

Analytics

  • Measure which pages and features are useful, in aggregate.
  • Help us find and fix problems faster.

We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles or sell your data.

03 Managing cookies

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies from the banner shown on your first visit, and change your mind any time in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site working, such as staying signed in.

04 Contact

Questions about cookies? Reach us at our contact page or privacy@theeat.app. You may also want to read our Privacy Policy.
